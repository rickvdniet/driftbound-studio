# Policy & monetization checklist (per video, before publishing)

Check the current versions of the policies themselves:
YPP policies https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392 ·
Altered or synthetic content disclosure https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/14328491 ·
YPP eligibility https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851

- [ ] Distinct concept: a new environment, not a recolor of an existing project (check the concept "similar to" flag and the pHash QA)
- [ ] Scene-specific soundscape (different layers, seeds and balance, not a shared stock bed)
- [ ] Every asset has documented provenance and commercial rights (creative QA has no errors)
- [ ] Required attributions in the description
- [ ] Title, description and thumbnail describe the video accurately; no health or medical claims
- [ ] Duration gives real value (sleep or long-focus use), not inflated
- [ ] Synthetic-content disclosure decided against the current policy
- [ ] "Made for kids" answered explicitly
- [ ] Human creative approval recorded (final approval in the app)
- [ ] Uploaded private first and checked after processing

Never: fake engagement, view or subscriber manipulation, Content ID or review evasion, hiding required disclosures,
reusing other creators' content.

Combining stock audio with one AI image does **not** make a video eligible. Commercial rights and technical quality
do not guarantee YPP acceptance. Monetization is applied for and managed manually in YouTube Studio.
Check the current YPP inactivity rules after monetization (the channel must stay active).
